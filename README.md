## Auction Platform Website

### Roles in the Project:

1.User: Can only view ongoing bids. <br>
2.Bidder: Can place bids on any product they want to buy. <br>
3.Auctioneer: Creates auctions with a timeline and sets the starting price.
4.Super Admin: Oversees the entire platform.

### Auction Process:

Auctioneer creates an auction with a start time, end time, and initial price. <br>
Bidders can bid during the auction timeline. <br>
After the auction ends, the highest bidder is declared the winner.  <br>

### Winning and Payment:
The winning bidder receives an email notification from the system. <br>
The winner pays the final bid amount to purchase the product.
